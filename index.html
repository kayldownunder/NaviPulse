Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

KaylDownUnder built the NaviPulse app as a Free app. This Service is provided by KaylDownUnder at no cost and is intended for use as is.

NaviPulse is a trip and fuel tracker. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not sell your data. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and why.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following only on your device. We never receive a copy of it:

  • Trips: start and end times, distance, duration, average and maximum speed, start and end place names, your notes, and the route recorded during the trip.
  • Fuel logs: the refuel date, litres, price, and distance and speed statistics since your previous fill-up.
  • Settings: your display and unit preferences.

You can delete this information at any time by removing entries in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Location

The app uses your device's precise location while a trip is being recorded, to measure distance and speed and to draw your route. Location is used while the app is tracking a trip and shows a notification while it does.

Information Sent Off Your Device

To give a trip or fuel-up a readable place name (for example a town, bay or offshore platform) instead of raw coordinates, the app sends the coordinates of that point to the following third-party services over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • The Android system geocoder, which is provided by Google: Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de): OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy
  • MarineRegions gazetteer (marineregions.org): MarineRegions disclaimer

These requests contain only the coordinates being looked up, and are used only to find a place name. We do not send your name, email address, device identifiers or any account information, and we do not keep the coordinates on any server of our own. The third-party services may keep their own logs, such as your IP address, under their own policies linked above.

The app also displays maps using the Google Maps SDK, which contacts Google to load map data. Google may collect standard technical information, such as your IP address, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Bluetooth

If you allow it, the app detects when your phone connects to or disconnects from a car audio or hands-free Bluetooth device, so it can start and stop a trip automatically. It checks only the type of device. The device name and address are not stored or sent anywhere.

Exporting and Backing Up Your Data

You can export your trips and fuel logs as a CSV or PDF file. The file is only shared with the app or person you choose from Android's share menu. We do not receive it.

You can also create a backup file of your trips and restore it later. You choose where the file is saved using Android's file picker, for example your device storage or Google Drive. Any storage you choose is governed by that provider's own policy, and we do not receive the file.

If you use "View Route on Google Maps" on a trip, the app opens Google Maps and passes it a sample of that trip's route coordinates so the route can be shown. This only happens when you tap that button, and Google handles the data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Backgrounds and Personalisation

If you choose a dashboard background image, a resized copy is stored privately inside the app on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Android Device Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, the app's stored data may be included in your Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account and is not accessible to us.

Advertising, Analytics and Accounts

NaviPulse does not show ads, does not use the advertising ID, does not include analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not offer user accounts. Because we hold no account or server-side data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children's Privacy

NaviPulse is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.
